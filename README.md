## Project Overview

This project proposes a therapeutic strategy using **SELEX-derived L-RNA Spiegelmer aptamers** to target prion protein misfolding. The concept focuses on designing stable RNA aptamers that can bind pathological prion-related conformations and potentially interfere with aggregation.

The proposal integrates molecular biology, aptamer selection, binding validation, aggregation assays, and nanoparticle-based delivery.

## Biological Background

Prion diseases are neurodegenerative disorders associated with the misfolding of normal cellular prion protein into abnormal pathogenic conformations. These misfolded proteins can promote aggregation and disease progression.

Aptamers are short nucleic acid molecules selected to bind specific molecular targets with high affinity. L-RNA Spiegelmers are especially attractive because they are more resistant to nuclease degradation than natural RNA molecules.

## Aim

To design a therapeutic research workflow for selecting and validating optimized L-RNA aptamers that target prion protein misfolding and reduce aggregation potential.

## Proposed Workflow

```text
Target protein preparation
        ↓
SELEX-based aptamer selection
        ↓
Aptamer optimization
        ↓
Binding validation
        ↓
Aggregation inhibition assays
        ↓
Nanoparticle-based delivery design
        ↓
Cellular uptake and functional evaluation
```

## Proposed Methods

| Method | Purpose |
|---|---|
| SELEX | Select aptamers with high affinity for prion protein target |
| L-RNA Spiegelmer design | Improve nuclease resistance and therapeutic stability |
| SPR / BLI | Quantify binding affinity and kinetics |
| EMSA | Confirm aptamer-protein complex formation |
| ThT aggregation assay | Evaluate inhibition of protein aggregation |
| Nanoparticle conjugation | Improve delivery and cellular internalization |
| Flow cytometry | Assess cellular uptake of aptamer-based formulation |
